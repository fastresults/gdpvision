# Position Second Brain 2.0 as GDPVision’s core technology

## Recommendation

Avoid using “advanced technology” as a standalone claim. Name **Second Brain 2.0** prominently, then substantiate it with the capabilities already shown in the animation: live global connections, protected national records, traceable evidence, cross-Chamber use and government control.

This preserves an executive tone while making the technology feel consequential rather than promotional.

## Proposed slide copy

**Section label**  
04 · At the core · Second Brain 2.0

**Title**  
At GDPVision’s core is Second Brain 2.0.

**Supporting copy**  
An advanced sovereign intelligence layer that connects live global sources with protected national records. It organises evidence across the ten Chambers, carries the source, method, confidence and date of every figure, and keeps sensitive data under government control—so Cabinet can work from evidence that is current, explainable and ready for decision.

## Why this approach

- **“At GDPVision’s core”** establishes the technology as the platform’s foundation.
- **“Sovereign intelligence layer”** distinguishes it from a generic AI assistant.
- The next sentence proves the “2.0” claim through concrete capabilities rather than hype.
- The final clause returns immediately to Cabinet value, preserving the presentation’s business-case narrative.
- The existing diagram remains the visual proof: global sources enter from one side, protected state data remains inside the sovereign boundary, and governed findings feed the Second Brain.

## Scope

- Replace only the section label, title and supporting paragraph on the Corpus slide.
- Preserve the existing Second Brain animation, safeguards, layout and presentation sequence.
- Verify desktop and mobile wrapping so the expanded supporting copy does not crowd the diagram.
