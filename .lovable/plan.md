## Context

"No AI framing yet." appears in `ThreatBriefCard` when a threat has no `brief.bullets`. The "AI framing" is a McKinsey-style briefing the model produces when a threat is created: labeled bullets (Exposure, Second-order effects, Fiscal channel, Historical analogue, etc.) plus grounding citations. It's the "so-what" summary that frames the threat before the user starts reallocating FDI.

The current copy is jargon and dead-ends the user. Users also hit truncation in several dense cards (bullets, ledger sector labels, action notes, citation chips) with no way to read the full text.

## Part 1 — Rename + rebuild the empty state

Replace "AI framing" phrasing throughout Chamber 04 with **"Threat briefing"** (plain English, matches what it actually is). Keep the small "Generated by AI" tag as a trust signal, not the label.

Rewrite `ThreatBriefCard` empty state (`src/components/studio/ThreatBriefCard.tsx`) to:

- Explain what a briefing is in one sentence: "A short, AI-generated briefing on how this threat hits the economy — exposure, second-order effects, fiscal channel, historical analogue — with citations to ground each point."
